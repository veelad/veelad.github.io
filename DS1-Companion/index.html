Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. DS1 Guide does not require an account or sign-in.

Where is my checklist progress stored?

Checklist progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. DS1 Guide does not provide its own cloud-sync account.

How do I remove ads?

On the initial Dashboard, tap the orange REMOVE ADS button below the circular category menu. The purchase screen shows the localized price for a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore a Remove Ads purchase?

Open REMOVE ADS from the Dashboard and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple or Google account that made the original purchase. You will not be charged again for a successful restoration.

How do I reset my progress?

On the Dashboard, press and hold the reset control for two seconds. The app asks for confirmation before clearing local progress.
