DragonWilds Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Summary

DragonWilds Companion is an unofficial game reference and planning app. The developer does not receive your saved progress or planning data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. Remote images and links connect to third-party services as described below.

Data stored on your device

The app stores checklist progress, craft-planner quantities and inventory, read state, and display preferences locally on your device. Images may be cached locally. This information supports the app's progress tracking, planning, and display features and is not uploaded to the developer. Operating-system backup and restore behavior depends on your device and backup settings.

Remote images and external links

The core reference data and calculations are included in the app. Uncached images may load automatically over HTTPS from the attributed RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki or its image-hosting servers. These requests expose your IP address and ordinary connection information, such as the requested image and request time, to those servers. The app does not include your saved progress, inventory, or preferences in these requests. Image hosts control their own logging, retention, and security practices.

Opening a source link connects your browser to the selected website, which follows its own privacy policy. The developer does not control these external services.

Email support

The feedback action opens your email application. If you send an email to veelected@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide, and uses them to respond to your request. Avoid including sensitive information. Email is handled by your email provider and the developer's email provider under their own security and privacy practices.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local app data remains until it is reset or removed. Settings → Reset Journey Data clears progress, craft-planner data, and read state while preserving display preferences. Restore Display Defaults resets display preferences. Android Settings → Apps → DragonWilds Companion → Storage → Clear storage removes the app's locally stored data and cache. Device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle your request and maintain related support records. To request deletion of correspondence held by the developer, email veelected@gmail.com. Third-party image-host logs and device backups are controlled by their respective providers.

Local data is held in the app's storage protected by the operating system; it is not a separately encrypted vault. Remote image requests use HTTPS. The app does not operate a developer server that stores your progress or planning data.

Children's privacy

DragonWilds Companion is a general game reference tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact the developer if a child has sent personal information so it can be addressed.

Policy changes

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the developer of DragonWilds Companion at veelected@gmail.com.

DragonWilds Companion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex.
