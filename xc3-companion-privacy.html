Privacy Policy — XC3 Companion

Last updated: September 4, 2026

XC3 Companion ("the app") is an unofficial checklist and reference app. It does not require an account or login.

Information stored only on your device

Checklist progress, archived compatibility state, display preferences, and spoiler settings are stored locally using the operating system's app storage. This information is not sent to the developer and can be removed from the app's Settings screen or by uninstalling the app.

Remote reference images and links

Catalog art is requested from attributed third-party wiki hosts when available. Like any ordinary web request, those hosts may receive technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and user agent under their own privacy practices. The app does not add an advertising identifier, account identifier, checklist progress, or other profile information to those requests. Opening a source link or the feedback link is a user-initiated action handled by your browser or mail app.

What the developer does not collect

  • No account, email address, password, or login
  • No analytics, advertising identifier, or behavioral tracking
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No purchase or payment data
  • No developer-operated backend or user database

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating the date above.
