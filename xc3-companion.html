XC3 Companion Support

XC3 Companion is an unofficial checklist and reference app. Progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Contact

For support or feedback, email veelected@gmail.com.

Reset local data

Open Settings in the app and choose Reset Journey Data. Uninstalling removes current local app data. Android may restore progress from an operating-system backup when you reinstall or move to another device; manage backup copies through your device or backup account settings.
