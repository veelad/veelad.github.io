DS3 Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Summary

DS3 Companion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your checklist progress, planning data, and display preferences are stored locally on your device. This information remains on the device unless you remove it through the app or the operating system.

Network access and external links

The app's core catalog and tools work offline. If you choose to open a source or support link, your browser will connect to that third-party website. Its privacy practices are governed by its own policy.

Children's privacy

DS3 Companion is a general-audience reference tool and is not directed to children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Policy changes

If a future release adds a service that changes these practices, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that release.
