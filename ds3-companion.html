DS3 Companion — Support

DS3 Companion is an offline checklist, quest guide, and planning companion for Dark Souls III.

Contact

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, email veelected@gmail.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Where is my checklist progress stored?

Checklist progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. DS3 Companion does not currently sync progress between devices.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings in the app and use the reset control. The app asks for confirmation before clearing local progress.

Why are some item images unavailable?

Item images load from attributed third-party hosts and need an internet connection when they are not already cached. If an image cannot load, the app shows a fallback. The core catalog, checklists, and planning tools remain usable offline.

Privacy

Your checklist progress and planning data stay on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. Loading remote item images discloses your IP address and normal network request information to the image host. Read the full DS3 Companion Privacy Policy.
